# Code of Conduct

Bridge is a small open-source project. We expect everyone interacting
with it — contributors, reviewers, issue reporters, discussion
participants — to behave like reasonable adults.

## Expected behaviour

- **Assume good faith.** People come at the same problem from different
  angles. Ask before accusing.
- **Debate the work, not the contributor.** Critique code, designs,
  and proposals — not the person who wrote them.
- **Stay on topic.** Bridge issues are for Bridge. Off-topic threads
  drift, attract noise, and bury the actual signal.
- **Be patient with newcomers.** The first PR is the hardest one to
  send. If someone's missing context, give it to them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, harassment, or insults — public or private.
- Discriminatory language or imagery: anything targeting someone's
  identity, background, beliefs, orientation, or appearance.
- Sustained disruption of issues, PRs, or discussions.
- Doxxing or threats of any kind.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will moderate discussions that violate these expectations.
Possible responses, in escalating order:

1. A private nudge to course-correct.
2. A public reminder on the offending thread.
3. Edit or removal of the offending comment / PR / issue.
4. Temporary block from the repo.
5. Permanent ban.

Report problems privately to **valknarr@pm.me** if you don't want to
raise them in-thread. Reports are taken seriously and handled
discreetly.

## Scope

These expectations apply anywhere Bridge contributors interact in their
Bridge-related capacity — this repo's issues / PRs / discussions, any
linked chat channels, and at events where Bridge is being presented.
